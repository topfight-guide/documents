# Topfight

Topfight is a highly competitive mode in Dewier's housing, Bridge Fighting. 

This gamemode uses the same principles as sumo and is closely related to pvp used in Ranked Bedwars (rbw).

The complexity of 1.8 pvp goes into a rabbit hole much deeper than most people realize. 

These articles are meant to help explain pvp mechanics and extra gamesense tips in-deptch for topfight.

Articles are organized by difficulty (easy->hard), but these are opinion based and may not apply to you.

These articles were also written by low - medium ping players (<100 ms), so some things may not apply to you as a higher ping player.

# How to Play

Topfight is easy to learn, impossible to master.

The core mechanics are simple:
Two players fight on a platform, with the goal being to hit the opponent off. 

This is the exact same as sumo.

However, the difference is the platform shape and size - specifically made to emulate rbw environments,

In a proper 1v1, the format is typically first to 10, with rounds taking roughly 2-20 seconds each.

To start playing, you must first join Dewier's housing.
On Hypixel, type `/home` followed by `/visit dewier`. This should send you to his housing.

Finally, navigate either the compass or the npc's for "Realistic Platforms"

# Fighting Etiquette

Here are a list of things to follow and understand when playing topfight.

- Never interrupt fights
  - If two people are fighting, don't third party. Topfight is a 1 on 1 game, so wait in line
- Skill Issue
  - Don't make excuses
- Your ping DOES NOT MATTER
  - If you are a skilled, you will become a great topfight player regardless of ping
- Try not to be toxic
  - Just don't
