# W-Tapping

W-Tapping is the best form of sprint resetting for pvp. It is easy to control and also very effective.

-# Sprint resetting is the act of stoping and starting your sprint again, dealing more knockback by abusing sprint hits

## How to W-Tap

After landing a hit on the opponnent, quickly release the w key and then hold it again. The w key should be held for almost the entire fight.

## When to Use

In general, w-tapping should be preformed immediately after every hit. Doing so deals the most amount of kb possible. This applies especially to every pvp mode (even in 1.9 too).

Despite this, it is often beneficial to not w-tap. If you are actively winning a trade, refraining from w-tapping towards the end of the trade can give you a free combo.

This is because continuing to w-tap will result in the opponent flying too away from you, making it impossible to combo.

This is an important skill to learn, as it allows to transition from winning trades to a combo-lock.

Knowing exactly when to not w-tap is a skill learnt with dediated time and practice, eventually becoming muscle memory. A good place to learn this skill is in the arena of Dewier's housing.

## Practice

If you are a beginner who is just learning w-tapping, learning on servers with a more controlled kb like minemen (na.minemen.club) can make it easier to train.

## Types of Sprint Resetting

Besides w-tapping, there is also s-tapping, blockhitting, shift-reset. Any form of stopping and enabling your sprint is also considered a sprint reset.

Even with all of these methods, w-tapping and s-tapping should be the only form of sprint resetting implemented in any given fight of almost any mode.

**W-Tapping**

- Release w for a split second. The time that w should be released for should remain minimal in all senarious.

**S-Tapping**

- Release w and hold s for a short period of time. The exact amount of time to release w and hold s can vary on the task formed.
- e.g. Someone backed up against a wall would require a longer s-tap.
- S-tapping is very risky because it kills all momentum, making it easy to get flawlessed if the opponent goes for a light hit select or first hit.

**Block Hitting**

- Block hitting is done by block your sword after every hit. 
- While block hitting by itself is a very important skill when it comes to other gamemodes, relying on it as a sprint reset method is not a good idea (especially in topfight).





For topfight, w-tapping is the only form of sprint-resetting needed, because other methods such as s-tapping kill more momentum.


## Summary

W-tapping is a relatively easy skill to learn, but it is also the base of which almost every other pvp technique relies on. Getting w-tapping down to muscle memory is a skill every pvper needs to advance further in topfight.
