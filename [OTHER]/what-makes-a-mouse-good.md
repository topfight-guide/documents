# SHOPPING/CHOOSING A MOUSE
Firstly, on a hardware level, you should be looking for:

- Polling rate options (1k-8k to minimize input delay; though sometimes high polling rate may cause abnormal cursor movement)
- Various DPI options
- Decent battery life


Next, you want a mouse that can fit your hand size and shape, and natural grip.
The goal is to have perfect mouse control to enable good tracking and adjusting abilities.

If you have questions about choosing a mouse, look through this forum and see others reviews on them. If it is not enough information, DM someone who is knowledgeable in aiming and peripherals.

# AIMING

If you are newer to the aiming community, and Minecraft is your only source of aim game, then you should be looking to find alternative ways to improve mouse control.
Precision & Flicking - Commonly people play Tactical Shooter games like Valorant or CS2
Tracking - Commonly people play games such as Apex legends, Overwatch, Fortnite, Quake, any movement game. Tracking is most important for Minecraft. 

Your aim will not improve quickly if you do not focus on it. Many players overlook it as something that improves naturally. If you watch top aimers and try to replicate the way they aim, you will ensure that you are always improving, even if just by 1%. Aim trainers such as Kovaaks or Aimlabs, even Oblivity (Dont use their sensitivity finder, trust me) are very useful for developing into an intermediate/advanced level of mouse control. DISCLAIMER - Aim trainers, while heavily useful for mouse control, have diminishing returns the higher ranking you get beyond the trainer itself. Specialize in a game, and your aim in that game specifically will improve drastically as you adjust to movement speed and patterns, given you have good mouse control already.

I recommend that newer players start on the heavier end of mice (~70g or higher), to ensure that they minimize shakiness in their aim, and to prevent over-gripping. 
For more advanced players and aimers, light mice are heavily preferred, especially for tracking scenarios. A light mouse offers the most control, given that the user has good enough mouse control to wield it.
