This server goes over many skills, on a surface level and in depth, for the purpose of topfighting ( ⁠topfight ). This channel serves to avoid any confusion for those who may struggle in navigating the server.

Before interacting in any way, make sure to read ⁠rules , and ensure you follow Discord TOS. 
To choose what pings you want to get within this server, whether for giveaways, coaching availability, server changes, or new information, go to the ⁠roles channel to use the bot, and be assigned what pings you would like to get.

Each category of the server is organized into sections, from top to bottom, based on the complexity and essentialness of them. It is recommended to learn skills from top to bottom as a beginner, ensuring mastery of them before moving to things outside of your skill range.
