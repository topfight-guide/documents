# Burst Clicking
Burst clicking typically branches off of butterfly clicking, and improves hit registration while giving the player control over their hit timings.

## How to burst click
To burst click, you must first be able to butterfly click first. When hitting an opponent, instead of single-tapping every click in your combo/approach, you will click in groups of 3-4 clicks via butterflying, or until you see your hit visually register. In practice, the goal is to replace any single clicking with bursts of clicks.

## Why burst click?

- Hypixel is known, especially for higher ping players, to give very poor hit registration. Hit registration failure is often referred to as "Hitreg." Hitreg is when, despite the player successfully hitting and sending an attack packet to their opponent, the server returns that the hit isnt registered due to poor latency. This is uncontrollable and can often lead to losing fights. Burst clicking significantly lowers the chance of hitreg happening, by clicking multiple times rather than a single time, ensuring that for each attempted hit, one of them actually goes through.
- Simply spam clicking during a combo on Hypixel will not cut it, as some hits must be delayed intentionally to hold a combo. Also, spam clicking endlessly can be tiring for most.
- Overall, this minimizes fatigue, and allows for controlled, precisely-timed hits without hitreg.
